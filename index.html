Reward modeling for music agents

At Spotify, I combined reward modeling with direct preference optimization (DPO) to train music agents from listening feedback. The approach improves tool-use plans using feedback on the playlists they produce. Production A/B tests showed 70% fewer erroneous tool calls and 4% more listening time.

Spotify Research article

Embedding-to-Prefix

Embedding-to-Prefix (E2P) maps existing user embeddings to a single learned soft prefix token, personalizing a frozen LLM without updating its weights. I developed the method at Spotify; it was used for long-term personalization in GLIDE, a generative podcast recommender.

E2P paper GLIDE paper

Models of conversational speech

I developed SpecialTime and Meeter to analyze spoken conversation. SpecialTime provides real-time feedback on dialogue acts in parent–child speech. Meeter uses transcription, sentence segmentation, and classification to measure information sharing and shared understanding in group discussions. My speech research also includes real-time emotion recognition on mobile devices.

SpecialTime paper Meeter paper Speech emotion paper

Image-grounded dialogue generation

With Microsoft Research, I developed dialogue models conditioned on visual sentiment, scene features, and facial expressions. Compared with text-only models, image grounding produced more informative, emotional, and specific replies.
